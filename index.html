RED ROOM

ABOUT RED ROOM

RED ROOM is a browser-based photo and video editor that adds a cinematic red filter and watermark to your media. Everything runs locally in your browser — no files are uploaded to any server.

HOW TO USE

  1. Upload — Drag & drop or click to upload a photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or video (MP4, MOV).
  2. Edit — Use the Edit tab for undo, cut/copy/paste, rotate, flip, crop. Set your brush size, opacity, and color.
  3. Effects — Apply red filter, B&W, sepia, blur, sharpen, vintage, and more. Each effect has an intensity slider (0–100%) so you can control how strong it applies.
  4. Tools — Draw with pencil, brush, or eraser. Use fill, color picker, text, clone stamp, magic wand, and advanced tools. Each tool has its own intensity/opacity control.
  5. Watermark — Toggle the "RedRoom" text, date, and time stamps on or off. All are fixed at 10% opacity and placed at the bottom.
  6. Download — Hit the Download button to save your edited image (PNG) or video (WebM) with all effects and watermarks baked in.

TIPS

  • Adjust the Effect Intensity slider before clicking an effect to control its strength.
  • Use Undo (header bar or Edit tab) to revert any change — up to 40 steps.
  • For Clone Stamp: Alt+Click to set the source, then paint.
  • For Text: select the Text tool, click on the canvas, type your text, and place it.
  • Video effects (red filter, vignette, watermark) are applied during download — preview shows them in real time.
